# Market Research Findings (provenance)

Generated by the `stock-research-grounding` web-research workflow (4 parallel analysts +
synthesis) to ground the fabricated fundamentals and tune the price engine, per the
project requirement to "use websearch to learn how stock research typically works."

## Equity research report structure
Summary/recommendation box (rating, 12-month price target, implied upside) → Company
Overview → Investment Thesis → Business Model → Management & Governance → Industry &
Competitive Analysis (Porter's Five Forces + moat) → Financial Summary (forecast
statements) → Valuation (DCF + comparables + precedent txns, "football-field") → Risks →
Catalysts & disclosures.

Rating scale: Strong Buy / Buy(Outperform/Overweight) / Hold(Neutral) /
Sell(Underperform/Underweight) / Strong Sell. The rating is driven by implied upside to
price target (typical band −30%..+60%, ~+12% typical).

## Metric interrelationships (used by generateFundamentals)
marketCap = shares × price; netIncome = revenue × netMargin; EPS = netIncome / shares;
P/E = price / EPS = marketCap / netIncome; P/S = marketCap / revenue;
EV/EBITDA = (marketCap + debt − cash) / EBITDA; ROE = netIncome / equity;
FCF = operatingCashFlow − capex. Typical net margin ~10%, P/E ~22, P/S ~2.5, ROE ~16%.

## Price-engine tuning (market microstructure)
Order-flow impact follows Kyle's lambda / the square-root law `I(Q) = Y·σ·√(Q/V)`:
impact must be **normalized by a reference volume**, has a permanent (~2/3) + temporary
(~1/3, decaying) split, and price mean-reverts (OU process) toward intrinsic value with a
half-life on the order of hours-to-the-game-length. Volume couples to volatility (MDH),
and volatility clusters (GARCH-like persistence ~0.97).

**Game-tuning note:** pure-realism per-tick reversion (κ≈0.0001, ~48h half-life) makes
research barely pay off inside one 48h session, so `shared/constants.ts` uses
game-tuned values (faster reversion, normalized impact) that preserve the *qualitative*
real-market behavior while ensuring good research is rewarded by game end. Final values
are verified by the simulation harness (compounders beat decliners; short-run
autocorrelation stays low).
